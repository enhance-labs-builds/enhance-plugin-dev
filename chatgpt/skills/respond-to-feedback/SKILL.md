# Respond to feedback

Do what each clear review comment on an Enhance canvas asks, on the canvas or in the source its prototype was published from. Reply to and resolve the ones you finished, and leave open the ones you could not act on.

## Tools

- The description of `feedback_read` shows how to read the comment threads on a canvas.
- The descriptions of `feedback_reply` and `feedback_set_resolved` show how to answer a thread and resolve it.
- The descriptions of `canvas_read` and `canvas_update_html` show how to read a screen and change it.
- The description of `prototype_publish` shows how to update a prototype from changed source.
